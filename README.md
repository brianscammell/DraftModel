# FortyNineNorth

A personal, single-user fantasy football analytics tool for my own Yahoo Fantasy Football leagues. Not a product, not sold, no other users.

## What it does
- Recommends weekly starting lineups for each of my teams from projected points and matchups
- Flags free agents worth claiming, with suggested FAAB bids as a share of remaining budget
- Keeps a season ledger auditing past projections against actual results

## Yahoo Fantasy data used (read-only, with my own authorization)
- My leagues and league settings (scoring, roster slots, FAAB budget)
- My team rosters and weekly matchups
- Available players in those leagues

The tool never adds, drops, trades or sets lineups through the API. I make every move myself on Yahoo.

## Privacy
- Data is used only for my own weekly reports
- Nothing is published, shared, sold or redistributed
- Tokens are stored privately and can be revoked from my Yahoo account at any time
- Follows the Yahoo Fantasy Sports API Terms of Use

Not affiliated with or endorsed by Yahoo or the NFL.
